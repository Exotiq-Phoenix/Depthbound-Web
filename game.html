THE WORLD OF DEPTHBOUND / 01

A journey shaped
by your choices.

A systems-driven action RPG exploring responsive combat, layered progression and the mysteries of a shifting world. Depthbound is in private alpha: the design is ambitious and the implementation is evolving.

01 / COMBAT

Every action
has consequence.

Timing, positioning and resource management form the combat foundation. Melee attacks, blocking, feedback and stamina expenditure have working prototype implementations, with broader weapon and animation systems under continuing development.

02 / DUNGEONS

Descend into
the unknown.

Procedural generation is a major engineering focus: modular layouts, deterministic seeds, critical routes, side branches and traversal across dungeon floors.

Validation and streaming remain active areas of iteration. The current work is not represented as a finished 100-floor system.
