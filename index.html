ARCANA

Magic is a language, not a list.

Constructs combine source, geometry, movement, intensity, stability, targeting, duration, behavior, domain and secondary effects. Discovery is not mastery: better control, efficiency, output and perception expand what the same magical principles can become.

  • Normal and mass telekinesis
  • Construct-driven Fire, Water, Lightning, Earth, Arcane and more
  • Staves and foci that genuinely alter casting behavior
  • Hybridization, rituals, barriers, traps, enchantments and player-authored techniques
